Private lessons in five instruments — for beginners finding their first notes and advanced students preparing for the stage.

01

Piano

Lessons are taught on our Kawai grand piano and cover classical and contemporary repertoire, music theory, sight-reading, and performance.

Every student gets a plan built around their pace, age, and goals — whether that's preparing for exams and recitals or building a lifelong love of playing.

  • Beginner — Ages 4 and up
  • Intermediate — Technique & repertoire development
  • Advanced — Exam prep, concerto study, performance
  • Adult learners — All paces welcome

02

Violin & Viola

Violin lessons follow the traditional European approach: strong foundations first — bow arm, intonation, and tone — then the orchestral and solo repertoire that makes the instrument sing.

Viola lessons follow the same path in the alto clef, with the warmer, deeper voice that sits between the violin and the cello. Students can start on either instrument, or move from violin to viola as they grow.

Violinists and violists work toward recitals, our school orchestra, youth orchestra auditions, and, for the most dedicated, conservatory entrance.

  • Early strings — Ages 4–7
  • Intermediate — Position work, shifting, vibrato
  • Advanced — Concertos, chamber music, audition prep
  • Adult beginners — Welcome at any age
Book a Violin or Viola Lesson
03

Cello

The cello has the warm, singing voice at the heart of any ensemble. Lessons build the physical foundations — posture, bow weight, left-hand frame — before opening up its full expressive range.

From Bach's cello suites to contemporary chamber music, students explore the instrument's whole repertoire.

  • Beginner — Ages 5 and up (smaller cello sizes for children)
  • Intermediate — Shifting, vibrato, thumb position
  • Advanced — Orchestral excerpts, solo repertoire
Book a Cello Lesson
04

Drums &
Percussion

Rhythm is the foundation of all music. Drum lessons develop solid technique, reading skills, and a steady sense of time that makes any musician better.

From rock and jazz to snare technique, lessons meet students where they are and take them further.

  • Beginner — Stick control, basic beats, reading rhythms
  • Intermediate — Styles, rudiments, kit independence
  • Advanced — Ensemble playing, chart reading, studio technique
